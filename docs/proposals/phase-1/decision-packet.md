## Approval mechanics

- Each row is answered by the owner as **approved as proposed**,
  **approved with amendment** (state the amendment), or **rejected**
  (state the guiding rule and a new proposal cycle follows).
- On approval, the corresponding `docs/pending-decisions.md` entries move
  from `PARTIALLY DEFINED` to `RESOLVED` with a pointer to the recorded
  decision, and `grammar-rfc.md` changes status from PROPOSED to ACCEPTED.
- Implementation of `xl-lexer`, `xl-ast`, `xl-parser`, and `xc parse`
  remains **not authorized** until a separate implementation directive is
  issued — approval of this packet constrains *how* that implementation must
  be built, not *whether* it may start.
